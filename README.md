# Driftwater: First-Person Fishing

A small, atmospheric first-person fishing game that runs in the browser. I sit in my boat, read the water, cast beyond the wake, and reel in fish while the lake drips and shimmers around me.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. I can use:

- **WASD** to move and look around
- **Space** to cast or stow my line
- **R** to reel
- **W** to throttle the boat
- The on-screen controls on touch devices

The experience is intentionally lightweight and self-contained: the canvas draws the water, islands, boat, rain, bobber, and first-person rod without external game engines or build steps.
